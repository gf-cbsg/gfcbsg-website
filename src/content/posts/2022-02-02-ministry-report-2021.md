Below is the report our group sent to Faith Evangelical Free Church about our ministry in 2021.

## Goals

- Discipleship training of Chinese Christians in Grand Forks.
- Learning the Bible together to better understand God's word.
- Spreading the gospel to Chinese non-believers, with a focus on Chinese students, faculty and staff at UND.

## Highlights

- Four people accepted Jesus Christ as their personal savior: two from UND and two visitors from China.
- We hosted a mission team from California. Together with our co-workers, the team visited 12 non-believers individually, including five professors, five PhD students and two postdoctoral researchers at UND. Most of the visits took place in their homes, and the gospel was well spread, led by the Holy Spirit.
- We held an online memorial gathering for Prof. Ta-pin Chen, the founder of our Bible study group. Many former members around the world attended and shared the group's history and stories. We were greatly encouraged to see God's work among us over the past 20 years, especially as the group started with three believers.
- We invited a guest speaker, Pastor Douglas Vaviosky (Li), on August 13 and 14. Pastor Li preached a gospel message on "Walking on water" on Friday evening, and his wife taught us how to pray on Saturday morning. Several other speakers also preached online for our group.
- We held a BBQ at the university on Sunday, August 29, for new students, faculty, staff and their families. More than 40 people came and made friends with our group.
- We continued weekly Bible study in two groups. One group, for believers, studies in Chinese and covered Numbers, Acts and part of Kings. The other, for non-believers, studies in English and covered Mark and Genesis.
- Our co-workers met three times to discuss our ministry.

## Challenges

- The number of new Chinese students at UND has decreased significantly over the past two years.
