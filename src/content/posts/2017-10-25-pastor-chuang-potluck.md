Grand Forks Chinese Bible Study Group is holding a potluck to welcome Pastor Chuang this Friday night at Faith Evangelical Free Church. Everyone is welcome to join us.

Detailed agenda is in the following:

**Friday night session:**

- 6:30pm: Potluck dinner
- 8:00pm: Evangelic sermon
- 9:00pm: Q&A/individual conversation
- 9:30pm: Adjourn

**Saturday Morning session:**

- 10:00am–11:00am: disciple consolidation sermon
- 11:00am–12:00pm: Q&A or individual conversation
- 12:00pm–1:00pm: Lunch

Address: 1400 24th Ave S, Grand Forks, ND 58201 ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Faith%20Evangelical%20Free%20Church%2C%201400%2024th%20Ave%20S%2C%20Grand%20Forks%2C%20ND%2058201))
