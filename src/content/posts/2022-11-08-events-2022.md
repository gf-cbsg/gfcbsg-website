Gatherings and events of our Bible study group in 2022:

1. **Jan 7 – May 20:** Bible study of 1 Kings on Zoom; **May 27 – Nov 25:** 2 Kings.
2. **Apr 1–2:** Prof. Kwok visited. We resumed in-person meetings and had a potluck.
   - Gospel message: "The Impact of Jesus Christ on Human History"
   - Revival message: "Learning from Jesus Christ: A Mission-Driven Life" (Luke 2:48–49)
3. **Apr 8 and Apr 22 (Fridays):** a two-part workshop by Pastor Anton Van Straaten, Discipleship Pastor of Faith Evangelical Free Church (FEFC): "Cutting It Straight" and "Christ-Centered Bible Believers of the Word".
4. **May 6:** potluck at FEFC with a gospel message by Pastor Zhang Yuanlai, "For God So Loved the World" (John 3:16). **May 7:** discipleship training on the second coming of Christ (Matthew 24:36).
5. **Jun 3:** guest speaker on Zoom, Prof. Xiaowei Dong: "How beautiful it is to study the word of God".
6. **Aug 20:** welcome barbecue at University Park.
7. **Aug 26:** "The Power of God's Word" by Dr. James Tour (online, on YouTube).
8. **Sep 15:** the short-term mission team from California shared the gospel with 13 students and faculty family members at the UND Memorial Union.
9. **Sep 16:** potluck at Valley Christian Church (VCC), with testimonies from the California team and a gospel message by Tianhao Zhang, "The Right Choice" (Luke 10:41–42).
10. **Sep 17:** discipleship training with the California team on Nehemiah chapter 1.
11. **Oct 14:** Brother Li Dahui on the history of the Twin Ports Chinese Christian Fellowship (Zoom).
12. **Oct 21:** potluck and gospel message at VCC by Pastor Zhang Yuanlai, "Why We Need Faith" (Proverbs 6:23).
13. **Oct 22:** discipleship training on the 20-year history of the Red River Valley Chinese Christian Church (RRVCCC), shared by Brother Shaowu Zhao, a deacon of RRVCCC.
14. **Oct 28:** "Church Planting: Are You Ready?" (John 15:26–27, Matthew 28:18–20) by Brother Yuepeng Deng, founder of All-nations Bible Church (Zoom).
15. **Nov 4:** guest speaker on Zoom, Pastor Deyi Wei of Winnipeg Chinese Alliance Church: "See God's call to you clearly, and walk on the path of blessing by faith" (Genesis 12:1–9).
16. **Nov 11:** potluck and gospel message at VCC by Brother Shengming Yang of RRVCCC, "Who Is Jesus?".
17. **Nov 12:** discipleship training by Pastor Zhang Yuanlai of RRVCCC: "Acts 11: How the Church Was Established".
